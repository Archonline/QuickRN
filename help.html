Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Answers about practice modes, progress, study guidance, and beta limitations.

What is QuickRN?
QuickRN is an independent NCLEX-RN preparation and practice platform. It provides practice questions, exam-style activities, study planning, and performance summaries to support learning. It is not an official NCLEX examination platform.
What is the difference between the practice modes?
Self Study is intended for flexible question practice and review. Timed Practice adds a time-focused workflow. Predictor and CAT modes provide adaptive-style practice and performance estimates. These are learning tools; their behavior and scoring should not be interpreted as an official NCLEX result.
Are all questions and NGN interactions fully validated?
QuickRN is in beta. Question formats and interactions are being tested across exam modes, and some items may contain wording, formatting, scoring, or rationale errors. Do not assume every question has been independently reviewed by a nursing educator. Please report any suspected issue with the question identifier or a brief description, without including personal or patient information.
How do I report a question problem?
Use the “Report a question issue” control while the question is open. Choose the problem type and describe what happened. QuickRN saves a local backup and opens a prefilled GitHub issue in a new tab; review the details and select “Submit new issue” to send it to the QuickRN team. You can also download the local report copy. Do not include passwords, account credentials, or patient-identifiable information.
Does a passing probability guarantee that I will pass the NCLEX?
No. Any readiness or passing-likelihood display is an educational estimate based on available practice data and the platform's current model. It is not a validated prediction, a psychometric assessment, or a guarantee of NCLEX performance. Estimates may be unavailable or unreliable when there is too little practice history.
How does Client Needs mastery work?
QuickRN groups recorded practice attempts into broad NCLEX Client Needs areas to help identify topics that may need more review. Classification depends on the information available in each question, so some items may be unclassified or assigned imperfectly. Use the results as a study guide, not as a formal competency assessment.
Is the Clinical Study Coach a live AI assistant?
The current shared Clinical Study Coach uses local, rule-based guidance. It does not connect to an external generative AI service. Its suggestions are general study prompts and should be checked against current textbooks, course materials, and authoritative clinical references.
Where are my study progress and calendar activities saved?
Several study features currently save data in browser storage on the device and browser you are using. This means progress, saved questions, and calendar activities may not automatically sync across devices or browsers. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or changing devices may make locally stored information unavailable. Do not use QuickRN as the only copy of important study records.
Does QuickRN provide official NCLEX registration or NCSBN materials?
No. QuickRN is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NCSBN or nursing regulatory bodies. For registration, current exam policies, and official test-plan information, consult the relevant nursing regulatory body and the official NCLEX resources.
How do I report a bug or incorrect question?
For a question-specific issue, use “Report a question issue” while the item is open; review and submit the prefilled GitHub issue, and keep the downloaded or browser-saved backup if needed. For general technical bugs, use the GitHub issue tracker only for non-sensitive, reproducible problems. Never upload personal information, passwords, or patient details.
Can I use QuickRN for real patient-care decisions?
No. QuickRN is for education and exam preparation only. It is not a clinical decision-support system and must not be used to diagnose, treat, triage, or make decisions about actual patients.

Practice items and rationales may be incomplete, outdated, ambiguous, or incorrect. Clinical recommendations can change. Verify important information with current evidence-based sources, official exam materials, and qualified nursing educators.

Practice scores and adaptive-style estimates are not official NCLEX scoring, pass/fail decisions, or validated forecasts. Performance depends on question quality, available data, and implementation limits.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Describes the current beta implementation

1. Information processed

Depending on how you use QuickRN, information may include your sign-in email and authentication identifiers; practice answers and exam summaries; saved questions, notes, bookmarks, calendar entries, and feedback reports; and technical information processed by the browser or third-party services. Do not enter patient-identifiable information or confidential clinical data.

2. Where information is stored

The current exam, analytics, bookmarks, notes, calendar, and feedback workflows primarily use browser local storage on the device and browser profile in use. These records are not automatically synchronized between devices. The feedback form saves reports locally; it does not automatically send them to the QuickRN team. The Reports page reads locally saved reports and supports export. The current code inspected for this beta does not implement a Firestore-based cloud database for these study records.

3. Authentication and analytics providers

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication configured for email/password and Google sign-in. The sign-in page also initializes Firebase Analytics with the project's configured measurement ID. Google/Firebase may process authentication and analytics information under their own privacy terms and settings. Review Google's and Firebase's privacy documentation for details about their processing, retention, and controls.

4. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate access, maintain practice progress in the current browser, calculate performance summaries, display saved questions and notes, support calendar planning, and help diagnose reported defects. Do not assume that locally saved feedback has reached a project administrator unless you explicitly export and transmit it through the private beta feedback channel.

5. Sharing and third-party services

QuickRN does not currently provide an automatic cross-user feedback inbox or cloud synchronization for the listed study records. Information may be processed by Firebase Authentication and Firebase Analytics, and by external resources you choose to open, such as YouTube. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy notices. The project does not claim that third-party services never collect device or usage information.

6. Retention and deletion

Browser-local records generally remain until you clear the relevant site data, use the app's available deletion controls, or the browser removes them. Account credentials and authentication records are handled by Firebase Authentication under its policies. The current beta does not offer a complete in-app workflow to export or erase every category of account and provider data; use the relevant provider controls and contact the beta coordinator through the designated private channel for account-related requests.

7. Security and shared devices

Browser storage is associated with the browser profile and may be accessible to other people using the same device/profile. Use a private device, sign out when finished, protect your device, and avoid storing sensitive information in notes or feedback. No internet service or local storage mechanism can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children and sensitive information

QuickRN is designed as a nursing education tool, not as a repository for patient records or sensitive health information. Do not submit information about patients or other individuals. If you believe personal information was submitted inadvertently, stop sharing it and use the private beta support channel to request guidance.

9. Policy changes

This notice may be updated as the application, integrations, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

This is a description of the current inspected beta code, not a certification of legal compliance. Before public launch, confirm the Firebase/Google data-processing settings, retention, regional requirements, contact details, and user-rights workflow with qualified privacy counsel.

Session recovery and offline use

If you refresh or reopen an exam page during an unfinished session, QuickRN can offer to restore the saved question order, current item, answers, notes, bookmarks, and timer state. An unpaused timed exam continues to count down while the page is closed; pause the exam before leaving if you need to stop its clock. Progress is stored in this browser only and does not sync to another device.

Keyboard shortcuts: Alt + Left Arrow moves to the previous item, Alt + Right Arrow moves forward, and Ctrl/Command + Enter submits the current response. These shortcuts are disabled while typing in a text field.

If the browser reports that storage is full or unavailable, export a data backup and free browser storage. Offline notices indicate that new files or external learning resources may not load until the connection returns.
