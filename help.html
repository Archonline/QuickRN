Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Answers about practice modes, progress, study guidance, and beta limitations.

What is QuickRN?
QuickRN is an independent NCLEX-RN preparation and practice platform. It provides practice questions, exam-style activities, study planning, and performance summaries to support learning. It is not an official NCLEX examination platform.
What is the difference between the practice modes?
Self Study is intended for flexible question practice and review. Timed Practice adds a time-focused workflow. Predictor and CAT modes provide adaptive-style practice and performance estimates. These are learning tools; their behavior and scoring should not be interpreted as an official NCLEX result.
Are all questions and NGN interactions fully validated?
QuickRN is in beta. Question formats and interactions are being tested across exam modes, and some items may contain wording, formatting, scoring, or rationale errors. Do not assume every question has been independently reviewed by a nursing educator. Please report any suspected issue with the question identifier or a brief description, without including personal or patient information.
How do I report a question problem?
Use the Report this question control during CAT, Predictor, or Timed Practice, or the report option in Self Study. Choose the issue type and describe the problem. Reports are saved in this browser only; they are not automatically sent to the QuickRN team. Export your QuickRN backup or individual report file and include it when contacting the team. Never include patient or sensitive personal information.
How do I back up or restore my study data?
From the dashboard, choose Export backup and save the JSON file somewhere secure. To restore it, choose Restore backup and select a QuickRN backup file. Restoring replaces matching QuickRN data stored in the current browser; a safety backup is downloaded first. Backups are not encrypted or uploaded to QuickRN, so keep them in a private, secure location.
Does a passing probability guarantee that I will pass the NCLEX?
No. Any readiness or passing-likelihood display is an educational estimate based on available practice data and the platform's current model. It is not a validated prediction, a psychometric assessment, or a guarantee of NCLEX performance. Estimates may be unavailable or unreliable when there is too little practice history.
How does Client Needs mastery work?
QuickRN groups recorded practice attempts into broad NCLEX Client Needs areas to help identify topics that may need more review. Classification depends on the information available in each question, so some items may be unclassified or assigned imperfectly. Use the results as a study guide, not as a formal competency assessment.
Is the Clinical Study Coach a live AI assistant?
The current shared Clinical Study Coach uses local, rule-based guidance. It does not connect to an external generative AI service. Its suggestions are general study prompts and should be checked against current textbooks, course materials, and authoritative clinical references.
Where are my study progress and calendar activities saved?
Several study features currently save data in browser storage on the device and browser you are using. This means progress, saved questions, and calendar activities may not automatically sync across devices or browsers. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or changing devices may make locally stored information unavailable. Do not use QuickRN as the only copy of important study records.
Does QuickRN provide official NCLEX registration or NCSBN materials?
No. QuickRN is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NCSBN or nursing regulatory bodies. For registration, current exam policies, and official test-plan information, consult the relevant nursing regulatory body and the official NCLEX resources.
How do I report a bug or incorrect question?
Use the QuickRN GitHub issue form to report a reproducible problem. Include the page or practice mode, steps to reproduce, and a question ID if available. Never include passwords, account credentials, patient details, or other sensitive personal information.
Can I use QuickRN for real patient-care decisions?
No. QuickRN is for education and exam preparation only. It is not a clinical decision-support system and must not be used to diagnose, treat, triage, or make decisions about actual patients.

Disclaimer & beta limitations

Important information about the intended use and current state of QuickRN.

Educational use only — not medical advice

QuickRN is designed solely to support independent study and exam preparation. Its questions, explanations, suggested actions, study plans, scores, and estimates are not medical advice, clinical protocols, or a substitute for professional judgment, supervised clinical education, or authoritative references. Never use the platform to guide care for a real patient.

Question and rationale accuracy

Practice items and rationales may be incomplete, outdated, ambiguous, or incorrect. Clinical recommendations can change. Verify important information with current evidence-based sources, official exam materials, and qualified nursing educators.

Scores and readiness estimates

Practice scores and adaptive-style estimates are not official NCLEX scoring, pass/fail decisions, or validated forecasts. Performance depends on question quality, available data, and implementation limits.

Beta software

Features may change, be unavailable, or contain defects. Data may be lost or reset. Keep independent copies of important notes and study plans, and report problems when you encounter them.

Privacy and browser storage

Some study data is stored locally in your browser and may not sync between devices. Shared devices and browser profiles can expose locally saved information to other users of that device. Do not enter patient-identifying information, confidential clinical records, or sensitive personal data.

Third-party resources

External links, including videos and reference websites, are provided for convenience. QuickRN does not control third-party content, availability, privacy practices, or accuracy.

No official affiliation

QuickRN is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN), the NCLEX program, or any nursing regulatory authority.

By using QuickRN, you acknowledge that this is a beta educational tool and agree to independently verify clinical and exam-related information. This notice does not replace applicable privacy terms or legal notices.
