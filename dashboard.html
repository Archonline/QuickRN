What's New

QuickRN product updates · November 4, 2026

Latest updates
Expanded Learning Library

Learning resources are being organized around the four major NCLEX Client Needs categories and their eight subcategories, alongside body systems and clinical specialties. Topic pages bring together lessons, video resources, study notes, and focused review.

Learning videos and study resources

The Learning Center includes topic-based video discovery, embedded playback where available, and a report option for unavailable videos. Students can keep study notes alongside their learning.

NCLEX question-bank expansion

The question bank is being expanded toward the 2,000-item release target in structured batches. CAT, Predictor, timed practice, and Self Study use the shared question-bank pipeline; question counts reflect items actually loaded by the application.

Exam experience improvements

Exam pages share a consistent clinical case workspace, clearer question-type rendering, answer feedback, and rationales. CAT and Predictor entry flows have been refined to avoid blank question screens when a session starts.

Dashboard and progress tracking

The dashboard brings together answered-question totals, CAT and Predictor history, performance trends, and Client Needs priorities to help learners identify what to review next.

Development status

QuickRN remains an evolving NCLEX preparation platform. Question-bank content and clinical rationales require ongoing review; practice readiness estimates are educational indicators and are not official NCLEX results. Some browser-local study records may not sync between devices, so retain backups before clearing browser data.
