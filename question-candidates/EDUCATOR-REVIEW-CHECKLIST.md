# Nurse educator review checklist

Use this checklist for every AI-assisted candidate before changing its status to
approved. Automated schema validation is a technical gate, not clinical approval.

## Clinical accuracy and safety
- [ ] The item is clinically current and consistent with accepted nursing standards.
- [ ] The keyed response(s) are correct for the facts and timing in the stem.
- [ ] The item does not introduce an unsafe, contraindicated, or out-of-scope action.
- [ ] Medication names, doses, units, routes, calculations, and laboratory values are checked independently where applicable.
- [ ] Any time-sensitive recommendation is checked against a current authoritative source.

## Item quality
- [ ] The stem is clear, self-contained, and free of irrelevant or contradictory details.
- [ ] The task tests one defensible clinical judgment or knowledge objective.
- [ ] Distractors are plausible, distinct, and not accidentally correct.
- [ ] The answer key matches the interaction data and required number of selections.
- [ ] The rationale explains why the correct response is appropriate and why each distractor is not.
- [ ] Language is respectful, inclusive, readable, and free of avoidable ambiguity.

## NCLEX/NGN alignment
- [ ] The format matches the intended interaction and the QuickRN schema-v2 format string.
- [ ] The item maps to an appropriate NCJMM clinical judgment function where applicable.
- [ ] The item aligns with an appropriate NCLEX-RN client-needs category.
- [ ] For a linked case study, all items use the same case-group ID, correct sequence, and consistent evolving clinical record.
- [ ] The item is original and does not reproduce recalled, leaked, or protected NCLEX exam content.

## Decision record
- [ ] Reviewer is an authorized nurse educator/admin, not merely the person who generated the item.
- [ ] Reviewer identity, review timestamp, and a meaningful decision note are recorded.
- [ ] Any requested corrections are completed and re-reviewed before approval.
- [ ] The candidate remains outside the active bank until the separate publishing process is approved and validated.

**Decision rule:** Reject or return for revision if any safety-critical fact, answer,
rationale, or clinical source cannot be verified. Do not use an AI score as a
substitute for educator judgment.
